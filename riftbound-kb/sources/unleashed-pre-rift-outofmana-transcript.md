### Vi — V Stands for Vicious (15:48)

**15:52** Last and some would argue least, we have Vi. Vi gives additional mobility by readying units if you assign three excess damage in a combat. This is a surprisingly tall order, but can be facilitated by certain key cards.

The included champion is Vi Peacekeeper, and it is so good. Ambush, along with the additional utility of stunning a unit when attacking, is so strong and an extremely powerful pushing tool. Unfortunately, the deck is themed around aggression and does a reasonable job of that, but running out of gas should be a significant issue.

The deck overall aligns with the hyper-aggressive style that's common with the fury/order domain pairing. Most of the units have assault, which makes them powerful pushing tools, but vulnerable to counterattack. Vi can get around this weakness by readying the unit and allowing it to move back to base, but only if the damage you're dealing far exceeds your opponent's might.

Despite a logical game plan, some of the cards are just inherently weak. Sharkling, Arena Kingpin, and even Inferna are good in some situations, but awful in others. The mid to top end units are pretty solid with Lord Broadmane, Ultrasoft Poro, and Yeti Brawler. But a card like Ultrasoft Poro seems contrary to the overall gameplay of the deck since you usually want to be slowly accruing value with the Poro.

Interaction is reasonable with Square Up making it highly likely that whatever you attack with, you'll be able to retreat back to base after you've attacked. Smite is a premium bit of damage based removal, and Heroic Charge is a premium combat trick. The battlefield again doesn't feel like it aligns with the game plan. Your units should be horrible when holding, so you're very unlikely to want to play units out directly to the battlefield.

There's quite a few logical additions to the deck. Vault Breaker at common should be quite strong and synergizes nicely with your legend, allowing a unit to conquer, ready, and then gank across to the other battlefield. Soul Harvest should be very good as well and will help you to craft a battlefield where your units can get in and deal three excess damage. Ride of Conquest should be a high priority inclusion if you open it.

You are going to be pretty desperate for card draw in this deck. In terms of domain pairings, mind seems like the most logical choice. You have debuffs with Eclipse and Moonlight Affliction, making overkilling trivial. Crescent Strike will let you attack into a battlefield with damage already dealt, meaning overkilling will be easier. And Sprite Burst as we've mentioned multiple times is just amazing. Otherwise, calm can provide moves and stuns which can synergize with Vi's strategy as well.

In terms of cuts, I think Arena Kingpin would be the first on the chopping block as it's a sizable tempo loss and vulnerable to a few key removal spells, namely Deadly Flourish. Heart of Dark Ice was not particularly good in limited from my experience, so I can't imagine Arena Kingpin will be much better. I'd also consider cutting Carrion Dredger unless I really didn't have any other aggressive units. I would also run blank battlefields over Valley of Idols.

Overall, Vi will struggle pretty hard to get her legend to fire consistently and in situations that have been damaging to your opponent. It does seem pretty interesting to pair with the mind domain for those debuffs, but if you don't open those cards, you may struggle to find another domain that pairs well with her. Still, in principle, her legend provides synergy with the aggressive archetype. So, with the right card pool, I'm sure she can make things happen.
